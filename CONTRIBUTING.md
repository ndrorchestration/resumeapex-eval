# Contributing to ResumeApex Eval

## Evidence and governance boundary

ResumeApex Eval is an experimental evaluation repository. Its benchmark protocols, rubrics, tiers, and governance terminology are project-local unless independently established by external evidence. References to DGAF, Agent Amethyst, Agent Apogee, NIST AI RMF, or other frameworks describe project relationships or alignment; they do not confer certification or external authority.

## What This Repo Contains

- Goldcanstaytoday three-layer evaluation protocol (Performance / Reciprocity / Amethyst-Apogee Meta)
- Public known-answer fixture and executable evaluation harness
- Dataset 1 evaluation documentation and provenance boundary
- Prompt/evaluation rubrics and reproducibility tooling

The public fixture verifies evaluator behavior and deterministic repeatability. It is not model-performance evidence.

## Attribution & Standards

- **NIST AI RMF** — project-local risk-management alignment/reference
- **DGAF Framework** — related governance/evaluation research track
- **Paul-Elder Critical Thinking Framework** — rubric/reference foundation

External framework references should be treated as mappings/comparisons unless independently validated by the relevant authority.

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue describing the benchmark gap, documentation issue, or evaluator improvement.
2. Fork and branch from `main`.
3. Scoring changes must update the applicable rubric/specification and tests.
4. Submit a PR with reproducible test or evaluation evidence where applicable.
5. Do not label a project-local result as certified, production-ready, statistically validated, or independently verified unless the required evidence exists.
